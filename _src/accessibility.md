# Accessibility statement

Regiontrace wants all people to be able to use regiontrace.com and the model at model.regiontrace.com. This includes people with disabilities.

## The standard

Regiontrace aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) publishes these guidelines. In the guidelines, the W3C tells designers how to make web content usable for more people with disabilities.

## Status

Regiontrace partially conforms to WCAG 2.1 level AA. "Partially conforms" means that some parts of the site do not fully meet the standard yet.

## Checks of the site

On October 21, 2026, Regiontrace checked these pages with axe-core 4.10, an automated checker, against the rules of WCAG 2.1 levels A and AA:

- the sign-in page;
- the Projects page;
- the Workspace, before and after a run of the model;
- the Report, Methods and Validation pages;
- the home page and the policy pages of regiontrace.com.

Regiontrace checked each page in the light and the dark color scheme. With the checker, Regiontrace found two problems. Regiontrace repaired both on the same day:

- On the Run the model button, the shortcut label had too little contrast with its background.
- On the Validation page, a keyboard user was not able to scroll a wide table.

With an automated checker, a person finds only part of the problems on a site. On the same day, Regiontrace also did these checks by hand:

- With only a keyboard, Regiontrace made a new project, added a region and an event, ran the model and read the results. Regiontrace marks each item that has the keyboard's focus.
- Regiontrace looked at each page at 200 percent zoom, and on screens 320 and 390 pixels wide. Regiontrace looked for text past the edge of the screen.
- On the map, Regiontrace measured the contrast of each kind of label against the colors behind it.

With these checks, Regiontrace found five more problems. Regiontrace repaired all five on the same day:

- In the bar charts of output by year and by region, you saw the parts of a bar only with a mouse. The parts are the direct, indirect and induced effects. Now you see the parts of a bar when the bar has the keyboard's focus or when you tap it. For screen readers, Regiontrace names the parts and the total of each bar.
- On the map, the names of counties had a contrast of 3.8 to 1 where they lie over a region. Now each name has a light outline, and the contrast is at least 6 to 1.
- On a narrow screen, three parts were wider than the screen. These parts were the menu at the top, the table of regions in the Workspace and the wide tables on the Methods page. Now you can scroll each of these parts in its own box.
- After you selected Add an event, the keyboard's focus stayed on that link. Now Regiontrace moves the focus to the new event's first field.
- In the searches for areas and industries, a screen reader did not know which result was active. Now Regiontrace tells a screen reader which result is active.

After the repairs, Regiontrace found no failures with the checker or by hand on these pages.

## Known limits

- Regiontrace did not test the site with a screen reader.
- On a narrow screen, you see only part of a long title in the title box of a report. You can move through the title with the arrow keys.
- Clerk, the sign-in service of Regiontrace, supplies the sign-in form and the account settings window. Regiontrace does not control the accessibility of these parts.

## Feedback

If you find a problem with the accessibility of Regiontrace, send an email to [hello@regiontrace.com](mailto:hello@regiontrace.com). Tell Regiontrace the page, the problem, and the browser and assistive technology that you use. Regiontrace answers within 5 business days. If you cannot use a part of the site, Regiontrace can give you the information in another format.

## Technical details

Regiontrace supports the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. To use the model at model.regiontrace.com, you need JavaScript. Regiontrace uses these technologies for accessibility: HTML, CSS, WAI-ARIA and SVG.
