Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Regiontrace is a regional input-output model for economic impact analysis. Regiontrace applies this policy to regiontrace.com and to the model at model.regiontrace.com. In this policy, Regiontrace tells you what information it collects, why it collects it, who holds it and what you can do about it.

Regiontrace does not sell your information. Regiontrace does not show ads, and it does not track you on other websites.

Regiontrace is not yet a registered company. When the company forms, Regiontrace will name it on this page.

What Regiontrace collects and why

Regiontrace collects only the information that it needs to run the model for you.

Your account

At this time, you need an invitation to make an account. For your account, you give your name, your email address and a password. Clerk, the sign-in service of Regiontrace, keeps this information. Clerk keeps your password in a form that nobody can read back.

Regiontrace keeps a copy of your name and your email address, and the time when you last used the model. Regiontrace shows your name on the versions of projects that you save. Regiontrace and Clerk use your email address to send you messages about your account, such as an invitation or a password reset.

Teams

If you join a team, Clerk keeps the name of the team, its members and the role of each member. All members of a team can see and change the projects of the team.

Your projects

Regiontrace keeps the projects that you save on its server. In a project, you save your studies: the regions, the events and their amounts, the settings and the results of each run. Regiontrace keeps each version of a project, with the name of the person who saved it and the time. Regiontrace keeps your projects so that you can open them again and share them with your team.

In a study, you can enter plans that are not public, for example a new plant before its announcement. Regiontrace treats each project as confidential. See When Regiontrace looks at your information.

API keys

In your account settings, you can make keys for the application programming interface (API). With an API key, a program or an AI agent can use the model for you. Clerk keeps your keys. For each key, Regiontrace counts the calls, the model runs and the refused calls in each month. Regiontrace also keeps the time of the last call. Regiontrace uses these counts for the monthly limit in the terms of use.

Technical information

Cloudflare carries all traffic between your browser and model.regiontrace.com. To protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare reads the internet address of your device (IP address) and counts your requests.

Clerk keeps the IP address, the browser and the approximate location of each sign-in. With this information, you can see your active sessions in your account settings, and Clerk can stop attacks on accounts.

The server of Regiontrace records the time, the page address and the result of each request. Regiontrace does not record your IP address on its server. Regiontrace deletes these records each time it installs a new version of the server.

GitHub hosts regiontrace.com. GitHub keeps the IP address of each visitor for security.

Your email to Regiontrace

When you send an email to Regiontrace, Regiontrace keeps it. Then Regiontrace has a record if you write again.

What Regiontrace does not collect

Regiontrace uses no analytics, no advertising scripts and no pixels that track you. Regiontrace does not collect payment information at this time.

Cookies and storage in your browser

Regiontrace and Clerk keep a small amount of information in your browser. Without it, you cannot stay signed in. Regiontrace uses no cookies for advertising or analytics. If you block cookies, you cannot sign in.

The information in your browser is:

  • cookies from Clerk, so that you stay signed in;
  • a cookie from Cloudflare, when Cloudflare needs one to tell people from automated programs;
  • the width of the panes and the blocks that you fold, so that a page looks the same on your next visit;
  • the address of the page that you opened before you signed in, so that Regiontrace can return you to it;
  • projects that you saved in this browser before Regiontrace had accounts, until you move them to your account.

How Regiontrace uses your information

Regiontrace uses your information only for these purposes:

  • to run the model and keep your projects;
  • to sign you in and keep your account safe;
  • to apply the limits on API keys;
  • to answer your messages;
  • to tell you about changes to Regiontrace or to its policies;
  • to obey the law.

Regiontrace can make counts that identify no person, for example the number of studies in a month. Regiontrace uses these counts to plan the site.

When Regiontrace looks at your information

Nobody at Regiontrace looks at your projects, except in these cases:

  • to help you with a problem that you report, after you give permission;
  • to repair an error that stops the site, when there is no other way, and then Regiontrace looks at as little as possible;
  • to examine a report of misuse under the terms of use;
  • when Regiontrace must do so by law.

Regiontrace gives your information to a government only to obey a valid legal order. Examples are a warrant, a subpoena and a court order. Before Regiontrace gives information, it tells the people that the order concerns. Regiontrace does not tell them if the law forbids it, or in an emergency.

If another company buys Regiontrace or merges with it, Regiontrace tells you before it gives your information to that company. Regiontrace also tells you before a different privacy policy applies to your information.

How long Regiontrace keeps information

  • Your account, your own projects and the counts of your API key use: while your account is open.
  • A project that you delete: Regiontrace deletes it at once. Regiontrace keeps a copy in the backups of the database for 7 days.
  • After you ask Regiontrace to delete your account: Regiontrace deletes your account, your own projects and your counts within 30 days. Regiontrace keeps copies in the backups of the database for 7 more days.
  • Projects of a team: Regiontrace keeps them with the team after you leave the team or close your account.
  • Records of requests on the server: until Regiontrace installs a new version of the server.
  • Your email to Regiontrace: while Regiontrace needs it to answer you, and then Regiontrace deletes it.

Clerk, Cloudflare and GitHub keep their own records under their own policies.

Your rights

Regiontrace gives these rights to every person, in every country:

  • You can know what Regiontrace collects and why. Regiontrace tells you in this policy.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information.
  • You can change your name, your email address and your password in your account settings. You can also ask Regiontrace to correct your information.
  • You can delete a project on the Projects page. You can also ask Regiontrace to delete your account and all your projects.
  • You can download each project as a file, and the events and the results of a study as a workbook.
  • You can ask Regiontrace to stop a use of your information.
  • You get the same service from Regiontrace when you use these rights.
  • You can complain to the data protection authority of your country or state.

To use a right, send an email to hello@regiontrace.com from the email address of your account. Regiontrace answers within 30 days. If you write from a different address, Regiontrace can ask you to confirm the request from the address of your account.

In your account settings, you can also delete your sign-in. With that button, you delete only your sign-in at Clerk. Regiontrace keeps your projects, its copy of your name and email address, and your counts. To delete these too, send an email to Regiontrace.

Regiontrace does not track you on other websites. Thus Regiontrace treats each visit the same way, with or without a Do Not Track signal from your browser.

Security

Regiontrace encrypts all traffic between your browser and its sites (HTTPS). Amazon Web Services encrypts the database and its backups. Regiontrace keeps all ports of its server closed to the internet. Cloudflare sends traffic to the server only through an encrypted tunnel. Only the people who run Regiontrace can get into the server and the database.

No method of security is perfect. If someone gets your information in a breach of security, Regiontrace tells you as the law requires. To report a security problem, send an email to hello@regiontrace.com.

Children

Regiontrace is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account. Regiontrace does not knowingly collect information about children under 13. If you think that a child gave information to Regiontrace, send an email to Regiontrace. Regiontrace then deletes the information.

People outside the United States

Regiontrace operates in the United States. If you use Regiontrace from another country, Regiontrace moves your information to the United States and keeps it there. Your country can give your information more protection than the United States.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, Regiontrace uses your information on these legal bases:

  • to give you the service that you ask for (a contract with you);
  • to keep the site and accounts safe, and to answer your messages (legitimate interests);
  • to obey the law (a legal obligation).

Changes to this policy

Regiontrace can change this policy. After a change, Regiontrace puts the new date at the top of this page. Before an important change, Regiontrace sends an email to each person with an account.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, send an email to hello@regiontrace.com.
